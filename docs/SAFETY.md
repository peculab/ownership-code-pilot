# Safety and responsible-use policy

This project assists research, drafting, and human review. It is not an autonomous persuasion or outreach system.

## Required safeguards

1. Use only public, synthetic, or explicitly approved and de-identified material.
2. Keep a human decision before any external publication or contact.
3. Cite the approved source behind factual claims and show uncertainty.
4. Make AI assistance transparent where context, policy, or law requires it.
5. Record model and workflow versions without storing personal conversations.
6. Provide reject and human-takeover paths.

## Prohibited uses

- Pretending an AI is a specific human.
- Generating deceptive testimonials, relationships, or identities.
- Covert psychological manipulation, harassment, spam, or discriminatory targeting.
- Scraping or joining restricted communities without authorization.
- Uploading contact lists, private messages, credentials, or confidential business data.
- Automatically sending posts, messages, emails, or calls from this reference implementation.
- Circumventing platform rules, consent, access controls, or rate limits.

## Reporting a concern

Do not open a public Issue containing a vulnerability, credential, personal record, or restricted material. Follow [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md). For a content or safety concern without sensitive details, open an Issue labeled `safety`.

Maintainers may reject or remove contributions that weaken these safeguards even when the code is technically functional.
