# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for contributors of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and respectful.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Credit others' work and disclose important limitations or conflicts.
- Respect privacy, consent, platform rules, and project safety boundaries.
- Help newcomers understand the project without shaming questions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, sexualized conduct, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, spam, deceptive participation, or sustained disruption are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or report serious conduct to the relevant platform. Report concerns privately to `peculab.ai@gmail.com`. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This project uses these standards in repository spaces, project events, and public representation of the project.
